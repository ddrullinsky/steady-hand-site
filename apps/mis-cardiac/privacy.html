MIS Cardiac

Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy policy

Effective: 27 September 2026

Summary

MIS Cardiac does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No network requests for content. All content is bundled in the app and works offline.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, health data, camera or microphone.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your device only: whether you have acknowledged the disclaimer, your bookmarks (Favorites, Upcoming case, To review) and your last-used view (Study or Reference). They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

External links

Reference entries link to published articles (for example on PubMed or publisher websites). Those sites open in your browser and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Patient information

The app has no way to enter or store patient information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
