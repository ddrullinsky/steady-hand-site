MIS Cardiac

Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy policy

Effective: 3 October 2026

Summary

MIS Cardiac does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The few settings it saves stay on your device.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No network requests for content. All content is bundled in the app and works offline.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, health data, camera or microphone.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • Whether you have acknowledged the disclaimer.
  • Your checklist progress: which steps, decisions, safety checks and bailouts you have ticked for each procedure. Use Reset on a procedure to clear it.
  • Your bookmarks (Favorites, Upcoming cases, To review).

The app never sends this information anywhere. It is included in your own device backups if you use them, and it is removed when you delete the app.

External links

The Sources list at the end of each checklist section, and the study guide, link to published articles (for example on PubMed or publisher websites). Those sites open in your browser and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Patient information

The app has no way to enter or store patient information, and checklist progress is not linked to any patient.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
